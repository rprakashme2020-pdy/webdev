A focused business website with a mobile-responsive layout, WhatsApp button, Call button, enquiry form, Google Maps, social links and basic SEO. Final scope depends on your content and requirements and is confirmed before work begins.

Is the domain included?

The ₹999 fee covers the website package. A custom domain is separate because domain prices vary. I can help you choose, purchase and connect it.

Is hosting included?

I will help set up suitable hosting. Any paid hosting or third-party charges, if required, are confirmed with you before purchase.

Can the website really be delivered in 2 days?

Yes, for the standard package, once I receive your logo, business details, photos and approval to start. Extra pages or custom features may need more time.

How many revisions are included?

Reasonable corrections to the agreed content and layout are included before launch. Major scope changes or new features may be quoted separately.

Do you provide maintenance?

Yes. Ongoing updates, content changes and technical maintenance can be provided as a separate support plan based on what you need.
